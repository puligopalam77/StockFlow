Private-master implementation
database/migrations/085_private_shop_masters.sql (manual, after 084):
- Nullable owner_store_id on products, categories, brands, customers and suppliers.
  Variants, barcodes, relevant product/location prices and tax settings inherit
  product authority. New master writes default to the selected owning store.
- Existing IDs, SKUs, barcodes, invoice and transaction references are retained.
  Original tenant-wide SKU/barcode/code namespaces are retained, not renumbered.
  Per-owner category/brand names can coexist without merging shop records.
- Legacy masters are allocated automatically ONLY where the tenant has exactly
  one store, counting inactive stores. No usage/creator/profile guess is used.
  Multi-store or no-store records retain NULL ownership and are quarantined from
  master lookup/search until an explicit owner decision. Authorized historical
  transaction snapshots and report movement totals remain readable.
- Restrictive PostgreSQL RLS, write triggers, guarded definer functions and scoped
  repository/API access enforce authority. Forged selected-store/tenant context,
  foreign master references and edits to another shop's shared catalog are denied.
- Sharing is explicit, read-only and audited by actor/reason. The tenant owner
  needs Company Settings EDIT and actual source/target shop membership. Product
  category/brand prerequisites must be shared explicitly; dependent revocation is
  rejected. Contacts are not implicitly shared with products.
- Quarantined NULL-to-owner resolution requires the authorized owner, selected
  target shop and a recorded reason. Already allocated ownership cannot be
  reassigned through this workflow. Historical IDs are preserved.
- Existing authorized owner switching/consolidated reports remain controlled.
  Registration-wide GST reports retain their separate permission; sharing is not
  a substitute for registration mappings or GST consolidation authority.

Screen: /Admin/ShopData. API: GET/POST /api/shopmasters.
Entities: owner_store_id columns, per-master *_shop_shares, master_sharing_events.

Operating Expenses F04 implementation
database/migrations/086_operating_expenses.sql (manual, after 085):
- Private shop/location/profile expense categories and payees, draft/save/post,
  incurred dates and amounts; partial/full payments on their actual recorded dates.
- Five manual methods: CASH, UPI, DEBIT_CARD, CREDIT_CARD, BANK_TRANSFER.
  Immutable signed payment/refund/reversal movements; linked reversal cannot be
  applied twice. Exact operation identity retries return the saved result, changed
  payload retries fail, stale versions fail, overpayment/negative net paid fail.
- Posted corrections retain snapshots/reasons. Current amount cannot fall below
  net paid. Cancellation requires zero net paid; cancelled expenses cannot be paid.
- Private PDF/PNG/JPEG receipt attachments up to 5 MB, signature checks, authorized
  download, no public upload URL. Audit retains file identity/digest without a
  second base64 copy in every event. No stock, invoice or eligible GST ITC posting.
- Current corrected incurred report, signed paid-date report and cutoff outstanding,
  CSV export, list pagination and filters. Formula-like exported cells are escaped.
- Cash Closing subtracts signed CASH expenses and counts expense movements through
  a narrow authorized aggregate function. Refund/reversal cash flows retain their
  own dates. Existing saved closing snapshots remain immutable; recount rules remain.
- Existing module/action permission engine, RLS and tenant/store/location/profile
  guards. Separate view/create/edit/post/pay/reverse/cancel/attach/export actions.

Screens: /Expenses, /Expenses/Create, /Expenses/Details?id=UUID, /Expenses/Report.
API: GET /api/expenses, /options, /{id}, /report, /export;
POST /api/expenses/masters/{kind}; POST /api/expenses/{id}/{command}
(SAVE, POST, CORRECT, PAY, REFUND, REVERSE, CANCEL);
POST /api/expenses/{id}/attachments;
GET /api/expenses/{id}/attachments/{attachmentId}.
Entities: expense_categories, expense_payees, expenses, expense_payments,
expense_events, expense_attachments, expense_master_events.

Remaining migration decisions and release limits
Actual multi-store historical ownership is unknown until the owner reviews each
quarantined case, including category/brand dependencies and deliberate shares.
The read-only review output is triage, not an allocation approval. Do not infer
ownership from historic sales or auto-share records to make an import succeed.
No production 085/086 execution occurred. Both are manual whole-file migrations;
stop writers, back up and review actual schema/data before separately approved
execution. Do not re-run on an already migrated database or rename live IDs.

Expense reports use current corrected amounts, not an as-recorded historical
accrual ledger. Paid dates cannot precede incurred dates; prepayment accounting is
outside scope. The expense report requires one selected location. Category/payee
labels are create/reuse records, not in-place historical renames. No automatic
bank settlement, eligible ITC, full accounting or audited net-profit claim.
Per-shop independent active-profile selection remains outside this slice; existing
tenant-wide selection does not reassign/merge ownership. Broader master-editor
UX and every historical deployment topology have not been independently piloted.

Mandatory release reviews still require actual evidence: receiving-corrections,
after-sales, shop-model, migration-deployment, production-recovery,
production-hosting, physical-devices, pilot-acceptance and usability-issues.
No hardware/production/pilot sign-off was fabricated. These implementation results
do not authorize production deployment or claim that P0 is complete.
