StockFlow evidence, documentation and competitive claims policy
Reviewed 2 November 2026. Scope: documentation/evidence, no deployment authority.

Order of authority
1. Actual current source and actual schema for the environment being described.
2. Executed automated tests and verified runtime behavior, with their precise scope.
3. Actual deployment, recovery and physical-device evidence.
4. The 2 November 62-workflow code-backed audit.
5. Earlier plans, feature wishlists and competitive reviews.
Investigate disagreements; do not choose the more optimistic statement. Source
or a migration file proves a defined implementation, not an applied production
schema. A test fixture proves its exercised cases, not every deployment/history.

Status definitions
Implemented: end-to-end source workflow exists for the described scope. Deployment,
hardware and pilot acceptance remain independent evidence fields.
Partial: useful implemented subset exists with material workflow/coverage gaps.
Missing: no corresponding business workflow found in the reviewed code/schema
evidence. A similarly named field is insufficient proof of the requested feature.
Defective: a reproducible incorrect result exists, with source/fixture evidence.
Deferred: explicit separate design/approval scope, not a silently promised feature.
No newly reproduced transaction defect was found during this documentation review.
New defects must be recorded and handled under the approved development task.

When reporting development work
- Name the feature and audit ID, route/API/entities and actual implementation files.
- Separate source inspection, fake-service HTTP, real PostgreSQL fixtures, mocked
  browser HTTP, real device/deployment tests and customer sign-off.
- Record exact command, date, candidate/source identity, environment/scope, actual
  result and artifact; do not mark an inventoried suite as executed.
- Give unresolved limits, manual migrations, deployed status and pilot status.
- Keep dated test results immutable. Documentation/help changes do not upgrade
  an older acceptance result to a new source fingerprint or production pass.
- Link the existing release runner and pilot records. A build is one gate only.
  Do not create another feature project or duplicate permission/profile engine.

Corrections to use in help, demos and support
- Profiles: Electrical, Bike Accessories, Clothing and Footwear. Serial/warranty/
  repair capabilities currently Electrical. No general grocery/fractional/expiry
  readiness claim. Profiles restrict capabilities and never grant permissions.
- GST: legal businesses, effective registrations/shop mappings, reviewed catalog
  rules, issued tax snapshots, working registers/notes and accountant CSV exist.
  GSTR period preparation, GSTR-2B eligibility reconciliation and filing remain
  unavailable. Recorded input GST is not automatically approved eligible ITC.
- New Sale: registered customer, one fully paid Cash/UPI/Debit Card/Credit Card/
  Bank Transfer method. Electronic reference optional, receipt confirmation
  mandatory. Cash change is optional; enabling it with short cash blocks checkout.
  Credit Card means collected money, not customer credit. No new credit/split UI.
- Historical credit/split/walk-in invoices and authorized later collections remain
  supported. Later collections and refunds have their own dates; they are not new
  invoice revenue. Payment reference/confirmation is not provider settlement proof.
- Q01: persisted quotations, immutable revisions/lifecycle/expiry/print/manual PDF
  sharing and checkout conversion exist; no stock/payment/invoice until successful
  final checkout. Automatic quotation WhatsApp/PDF delivery is not claimed.
- F04: operating expense drafts/posting, categories/payees, corrections, signed
  payments/refunds/reversals, private attachments, reports/export and Cash Closing
  integration are implemented in 086. Purchase charges remain separate. Reports
  distinguish incurred dates from paid dates and use current corrected amounts,
  not historical as-recorded accrual accounts. No eligible ITC or net-profit claim.
- F06: tax-exclusive saved-value management estimate, unknown costs remain unknown,
  pre-sale receipt evidence, returns and discounts corrected. Not authoritative
  cost layers, inventory valuation, selected FIFO/weighted-average COGS or net profit.
- I08/I10: receipt batch/date text and barcode label grouping are not lot balances,
  FEFO, expiry enforcement, recall or fractional quantity support.
- A15: browser/server drafts and retry recovery exist; disconnected final invoices,
  offline authorization/numbering and deterministic synchronization do not.
- A09: real synthetic fresh-cluster restore/application reads passed. Actual
  scheduled/offsite recovery, assets/secrets/login and production RPO/RTO are open.
  A checksum/file alone is not recoverability. No production recovery guarantee.
- A08: scanner input/camera and browser/Android printing exist. Physical device
  acceptance remains open; PDF/print dialog is not USB/Bluetooth driver integration.
- G10: persisted e-way preparation/worksheet and unverified external EBN exist.
  Official generation/updates/cancellation/provider credentials are unavailable.
  No numeric StockFlow monthly cap is imposed by this preparation module; genuine
  provider limits/fees/entitlement and live integration remain unverified. Do not
  market unlimited officially generated bills on that basis.

Official GST source checked for this task

https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/Creation_of_Outward_Supplies_Return_in_GSTR-1.htm
The guide separates entry, summary/preview and filing with DSC/EVC. StockFlow's
working CSV is not a filed return. Actual portal filing/official provider results
need real acknowledgements and their real status. No statutory rate, threshold,
deadline or business-specific applicability was selected in this task. Historical
rules compilations and product advertisements are not current legal authority;
verify contemporary official requirements and authorized API versions before any
new statutory implementation. Accountant acceptance remains required.

Remaining uncertainties
Current deployed build/schema and customer usage were not inspected in this task.
The local single-store audit observation is dated, not proof of today's production
topology. Original audit line numbers can drift; current register lists checked
source paths. Not all 62 workflows were re-audited or rerun. Native-language help
translations, physical devices, external providers, production recovery and pilot
sign-off are not newly verified. The earlier documentation-only update changed no
posting or ownership. The later implementation adds 085/086, private-shop controls
and expense/Cash Closing services in isolated environments, with no production
migration or deployment. Dated acceptance reports retain their original scope.

Client-demo wording
StockFlow has source-backed billing, inventory, customer, purchasing and
after-sales foundations, with selected automated transaction/recovery tests.
Quotations and GST working records are implemented; additional financial,
compliance and operational capabilities remain limited or under development.
Production/hardware/pilot verification is separate. No comparative superiority,
complete accounting, official filing or universal industry readiness is claimed.
