Operating Expenses F04 - implemented source scope, 2 November 2026

Requires manual 085 then 086 migrations before deploying this candidate. No
migration runs at startup. These migrations were tested on disposable databases
only; production deployment and owner/accountant acceptance remain unverified.

Use /Expenses to search and page by incurred date, /Expenses/Create to save a
draft, /Expenses/Details?id=UUID to post/pay/correct and /Expenses/Report to report
or export. Category and payee identities are created/reused within the active
shop/profile; choose a different retained record in a correction rather than
renaming historical master labels. Posting makes the expense reportable and
allows payment. Saving a draft creates neither inventory nor cash movements.

Incurred amount: current corrected POSTED expense amount by incurred date.
Paid amount: signed payment movements by their paid/returned/reversal dates.
Outstanding at cutoff: current corrected posted expenses incurred by cutoff less
signed payments dated through cutoff. This is a current-basis operational report,
not a historical as-recorded accrual ledger or audited net-profit statement.
Draft/cancelled expense amounts are excluded. Payment history is retained even
when fully refunded expenses are cancelled. Corrections retain version snapshots.

Cash Closing subtracts signed CASH expense movements. A cash refund/reversal
increases drawer cash on that recorded movement date. UPI/card/bank expense
payments do not affect drawer cash. Do not also record the same expense as extra
cash removed. Old saved closings remain immutable; later activity changes the
fingerprint and requires a reasoned recount.

Payments cannot exceed amount outstanding; refunds cannot exceed net paid.
Reversing a payment/refund creates a linked opposite movement and cannot be
repeated. Cancel only after net paid is zero. Paid dates cannot precede incurred
dates; prepaid-expense accounting is outside this workflow. All five supported
methods record manual outflow evidence, not verified bank/provider settlement.

Receipt evidence: PDF/PNG/JPEG, maximum 5 MB, checked signatures, stored privately
in PostgreSQL, downloaded through scoped authorization with no-store/attachment
headers. No public upload URL. Supporting files are not automatically trusted or
eligible GST ITC evidence. No invoice, stock, tax document or eligible ITC posting.

JSON mutation identities persist for unchanged retries across reload. After an
unknown result, use Retry unchanged request. For an unconfirmed attachment,
reselect the same file. The server rejects changed values using the same identity.
Concurrent operators must reload after a version conflict. Expense viewing,
creation, editing, posting, payments, reversal/refund, cancellation, attachments
and export use separate existing permission actions and database checks.

Current active profile selection still uses the existing tenant-wide engine.
Expense records are additionally private to store/location/profile; no profile
change reassigns or merges records. Consolidated general/GST reports retain their
existing explicit authority. The expense report itself requires one active shop
location. No general opening balances, full accounting or automatic settlement.
