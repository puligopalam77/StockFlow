# Receiving batteries and geysers

Use **Receive new stock** when physical goods arrive from a supplier. Saving and confirming a receipt adds only undamaged quantities to stock available for sale.

## Receive a delivery

1. Choose the supplier and enter their bill number, bill date and goods received date. Attach a PDF, JPG or PNG bill if needed (up to 5 MB).
2. Use **Select Product**, search by name or capacity, scan the barcode, or choose a quick-add product. Use **Add new product** only when the model is not registered yet.
3. Check each item's quantity and buying price. Repeating a scan of the same model increases its quantity.
4. Open **More details** to enter damaged quantity, discount, GST, MRP, batch and serial numbers where needed.
5. Check the supplier payment days, delivery charges, other charges and overall discount.
6. Select **Receive Stock**, check the confirmation, then choose **Confirm & Receive**.

If the supplier is missing, use **+ Add Supplier** beside the supplier dropdown when your account has supplier-creation permission. Enter the supplier details and select **Save Supplier**. Review any duplicate warning before continuing. The saved supplier is selected automatically, and their payment terms are applied to the purchase. Cancelling keeps your receipt unchanged. Saving a supplier alone does not receive goods or add stock.

**Quantity is the total that arrived, including damage.** For 10 items with 2 damaged, enter quantity 10 and damaged 2. Only 8 become available for sale. The supplier bill still covers all 10 unless you separately record an agreed return or credit.

For serial-tracked items, enter one distinct serial for each good item and each damaged item in their separate fields. Check the serial counts before confirming. A serial can contain up to 150 characters. Receipt quantities are whole numbers up to 10,000 per line.

The receipt and its stock records save together. After saving, the screen shows the receipt number and the quantities received, damaged and available for sale.

## Orders for future delivery

Open **Purchase orders & deliveries**, then choose **Create purchase order**. Enter the expected goods and save the order. An order alone does not add stock.

When goods arrive, open the existing order and choose **Receive all or part of this order**. Record that delivery against the same order. On the order-delivery form, good received quantity and damaged quantity are separate counts; follow its field labels.

Direct receipts have no Save Draft action. Order drafts stay in the separate purchase-order flow. An authorized staff account must confirm stock receipts.

## Starting stock

Use the existing starting-stock option only for goods already in the shop when StockFlow is first set up. Do not enter goods again if a receipt already added them.

## Find purchase orders and stock receipt history

Open **Stock receipt history**, search by receipt number, supplier or date, and open the matching record. Check its products, quantities and serial numbers. Opening or printing a receipt does not change stock.

If a save result is uncertain, check receipt or purchase history first and use the offered retry action. Do not start another receipt for the same delivery until you know the result of the first one.
