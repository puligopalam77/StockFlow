Billing adjustment prompts — 2026-11-02

The shared line-adjustment dialog previously repeated discount/price fields when
requesting a tax reason, even if a reason was already stored. Its tax-only heading
could also describe a form containing several different adjustment reasons.

Changes in src/StockFlow.Web/wwwroot/js/sales-create.js:
- Ask for missing reasons only. Keep visible inputs stable during typing; hide
  completed reasons when the dialog is opened again.
- Use Adjustment Reasons when multiple reasons are needed, otherwise identify
  the relevant discount, price or tax reason.
- Keep tax-only context to product/SKU and tax change. Use Configured tax if the
  original rate is unavailable rather than inventing a value.
- Remove repeated approval-status/placeholder-approver text from line context.
- Carry saved discount, price and invoice reasons into the existing manager
  approval request as text; do not request the same entry a second time.
- Keep discount and tax reasons as distinct existing payload fields. Existing
  server policy, permissions, approval checks and checkout validation unchanged.
